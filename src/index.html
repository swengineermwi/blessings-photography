Elena Blessings Founder & Principal Artist
Behind The Lens

"I don't just take pictures. I preserve feelings that outlive time."

For over a decade, Elena Blessings has traveled across Europe, Asia, and the Americas capturing raw human intimacy, couture fashion editorials, and architectural grandeur.

Trained in traditional medium format film and digital cinematography, her philosophy centers on creating organic atmosphere, allowing subjects to breathe naturally while capturing the subtle glances and unscripted magic.

We treat every assignment not as a photo shoot, but as an immersive editorial publication. Soft organic light, timeless color palettes, and unobtrusive storytelling.

  • Cameras: Leica SL2, Hasselblad H6D-100c, Sony A1
  • Lenses: Noctilux 50mm f/0.95, Otus 85mm f/1.4 Prime Optics
  • Film: Kodak Portra 400 & Fuji Pro 400H 120mm
  • 2025 International Photography Awards: Gold Winner
  • Featured In: Vogue Weddings, Harper's Bazaar, Architectural Digest
